Support Ciel Study

Last updated: 12 August 2026

Several people have asked whether they can chip in. You can, and this page explains exactly what that does and — more importantly — what it does not do.

A contribution buys nothing. It does not unlock questions, add Grand Tests, remove the daily limit, or give you anything a person who contributes nothing does not already get. If you want more access, the paid plans are the honest way to buy it — please use those instead.

1. Where the money goes

Ciel Study is run by one person, and it costs real money to keep going:

  • A server so the bot answers at 3 a.m. and not only when a laptop in Kerala happens to be awake.
  • Payment processing fees on every transaction.

That is the whole list. There is no team, no office, and no marketing spend.

2. What a contribution is not

  • Not a donation to a charity. Ciel Study is a sole proprietorship, not a registered non-profit. There is no 80G certificate and no tax deduction for you.
  • Not a purchase. You receive no goods, no service and no access in return. Nothing is owed to you afterwards.
  • Not a subscription. It is a single payment. Nothing recurs, and no mandate is created on your card.

3. Refunds

Because a contribution is voluntary and buys nothing, it is not refundable in the way a plan is — there is no service to withdraw and no period to pro-rate.

That said: if you contributed by mistake, contributed more than you meant to, or simply changed your mind, write to me and I will return it. A rule that ignores an obvious mistake is not a rule worth keeping. Paid plans have their own, separate refund policy, which this page does not change.

4. If you would rather not send money

This genuinely helps more than a small contribution does, and it is free:

  • Report a wrong answer. Use the flag button on any question. Every report gets read, and the fixes page lists what changed.
  • Tell me what is broken. Mangled text, a missing figure, a question that cannot be answered — send it via /feedback in the bot.
  • Tell one person who is preparing. That is the entire marketing budget.

5. How to contribute

Contribute through Razorpay

Any amount, once. You choose it — nothing is suggested or pre-filled, and no amount is expected.

Payments are processed by Razorpay. Ciel Study never sees or stores your card details. See the privacy policy for what is stored and the terms for the rest.
