Fixes & what's next

Last updated 03 August 2026 · 13 entries

If you report a problem, you should be able to see what happened to it. This page lists every issue I've fixed in the question bank, the bot and the payment flow — including the ones that were my fault.

Found something wrong, or want to suggest something? Send /feedback in the bot — that is the only place I track requests. You get a ticket number, you can check its status with /myfeedback, and you'll be messaged when it's actually fixed rather than left wondering. Suggestions go in the same place; if I build yours, it shows up below.
