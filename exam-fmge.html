The FMGE Grand Test in the bot

  • 300questions, 2 papers
  • 3 × 50sections in each paper
  • 150 mineach paper, timed apart
  • +1 / 0no negative marks

The paper follows the official NBEMS subject weighting, not our bank's mix: Part A (pre- and para-clinical) carries 100 questions and Part B (clinical) 200, so Medicine gets 33, Surgery 32, Obstetrics & Gynaecology 30, Community Medicine 30, Radiology 10, and so on down to 5 each for Psychiatry, Dermatology, Anaesthesia and Orthopaedics. Questions come from the whole FMGE, NEET-PG and INI-CET bank. With no negative marking, never leave a blank.

What a Grand Test feels like, step by step

  1. Choose the test. Send /gt and pick FMGE. Before anything starts, the bot shows the number of questions, the time, the marking and what a break costs, so nothing in your score is a surprise.
  2. The clock starts. Questions come one at a time with A, B, C, D buttons and a Skip. The time left is shown as you go. You do not see whether you were right, and there are no explanations, until you submit, the same as the hall.
  3. One way only. You cannot go back to a question you skipped or answered, so decide and move on. Closing Telegram does not stop the clock.
  4. Need a break? Tap the break button. Your clock pauses and 10 marks come off at once, about ten questions' worth. A break lasts at most 15 minutes, then the clock runs again whether you are back or not. In the real exam a bio break costs minutes you never get back; here you can buy them back, with marks.
  5. Between the papers. When Paper 1 ends, pick a 15, 30 or 60 minute rest, or start Paper 2 at once. The rest is not exam time: Paper 2's clock starts only when Paper 2 does. Coming back cold is part of the real day.
  6. The result. Your marks out of the maximum, how many were right, wrong and left blank, each section's score, any break deduction, and how you compare with everyone who has taken the same test.
  7. The part that matters. Go through every mistake with its explanation. Each one goes into your Revise list, so the bot brings it back to you later until you get it right.

A sitting you leave open is scored automatically once the time it could have taken is over. Your past tests are in /gthistory. Grand Tests come with paid plans; see Pricing for how many each includes.
