Kept completely separate

CMS is a different exam with a different mindset, so its questions never mix with FMGE, NEET-PG or INI-CET. Pick UPSC CMS in /myexam and every part of the bot gives you CMS only: random practice, subject practice, exam mode and the CMS Grand Tests. Students of the other exams never see a CMS question. Topic-by-topic practice is not offered for CMS yet, because the topic lists are built for FMGE.

The real exam

  • 2 paperssat separately
  • 120questions in each

Paper I is General Medicine and Paediatrics; Paper II is Surgery, Gynaecology & Obstetrics, and Preventive & Social Medicine. Check the current notification on upsc.gov.in.

The two CMS Grand Tests in the bot

  • Paper IMedicine 96 + Paediatrics 24
  • Paper IISurgery, OBG, PSM 40 each
  • 120 mineach, 120 questions
  • +1 / −⅓10 marks for a break

Each paper is its own Grand Test, so you can sit them on different days, the way you would revise for them. The subject split follows the exam's own papers. Every question is from a real UPSC CMS paper, so the answer is UPSC's key, not a recall.

Where the scanned question paper was hard to read, an option was rebuilt and the question says so in a Note. Questions that needed a picture, or where we could not confirm UPSC's key, were left out rather than guessed.

What a Grand Test feels like, step by step

  1. Choose the test. Choose UPSC CMS once with /myexam; after that /gt offers only the two CMS papers. Before anything starts, the bot shows the number of questions, the time, the marking and what a break costs, so nothing in your score is a surprise.
  2. The clock starts. Questions come one at a time with A, B, C, D buttons and a Skip. The time left is shown as you go. You do not see whether you were right, and there are no explanations, until you submit, the same as the hall.
  3. One way only. You cannot go back to a question you skipped or answered, so decide and move on. Closing Telegram does not stop the clock.
  4. Need a break? Tap the break button. Your clock pauses and 10 marks come off at once, about ten questions' worth. A break lasts at most 15 minutes, then the clock runs again whether you are back or not. In the real exam a bio break costs minutes you never get back; here you can buy them back, with marks.
  5. The result. Your marks out of the maximum, how many were right, wrong and left blank, each section's score, any break deduction, and how you compare with everyone who has taken the same test.
  6. The part that matters. Go through every mistake with its explanation. Each one goes into your Revise list, so the bot brings it back to you later until you get it right.

A sitting you leave open is scored automatically once the time it could have taken is over. Your past tests are in /gthistory. Grand Tests come with paid plans; see Pricing for how many each includes.
