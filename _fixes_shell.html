If you report a problem, you should be able to see what happened to it. This page lists every issue I've fixed in the question bank, the bot and the payment flow — including the ones that were my fault.

Found something wrong? Send /feedback in the bot. You get a ticket number, you can check its status with /myfeedback, and you'll be messaged when it's actually fixed rather than left wondering.

Reports are never identified here. No names, no messages and no Telegram IDs appear on this page — only what was wrong and what changed.
