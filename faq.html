About me, and why this exists

Why did you share your own score of 147?

Because I'm not selling you someone else's success story.

I sat the same exam and I fell short — 147 out of 300. I could have said nothing and let you assume I'd cleared it. Then this would just be one more account riding on a result to sell you something.

I'd rather be straight with you: I'm preparing alongside you, using the same question bank you are. I know exactly what it costs to stay inside that boundary. And I'd rather pick up where I'm weak than sit and drown in what I could have done.

Why only 5 free questions a day — why not make it free?

My first instinct, once I saw the bot actually worked, was to make all of it free.

I changed my mind for a reason I didn't expect: free gets devalued. Something that costs nothing tends to be treated as though it's worth nothing, and it wouldn't have helped anyone the way I intended.

Five a day is also a number you can genuinely absorb. Nearly every FMGE aspirant is already in paid classes, online or offline — five questions with proper explanations is something you can actually finish alongside that, instead of another backlog to feel guilty about.

If you want more, the price is a fraction of what comparable question banks charge. And the free five never expire — no trial, no countdown.

Do you plan to make money from this?

Not really. What I want is for it to pay for itself.

There are real costs — hosting, keeping it running, the hours that go into building it — and I don't think anyone's work should go to waste, mine or yours. The plans exist to cover the bill, not to turn a profit.

I'm nowhere near covering it yet. If that ever changes, the difference goes back into the bank: more questions, more figures, better explanations.

Why spend so much time on this?

Because I saw what it could do.

I built it while working on another project, and once it worked I realised it could genuinely change how someone prepares. That's not a small thing to walk away from.

My circumstances are my own and I'll keep them there. But as long as the people I love support me in this, I'm glad to keep going — and glad to share it.

Do you accept donations?

Not at the moment. I'd like to set that up, along with a page crediting the people who've helped — whether that's money, questions, or corrections.

If you've ever reported a wrong answer key, you've already contributed more than you think. Thank you.

Using the bot

Do you have to be technical to use the bot?

Not at all. If you can use Telegram, you can use this.

It's buttons and taps. You never have to type a command if you don't want to — tap, answer, read the explanation, move on.

Why Telegram?

Two reasons.

It fits how this is built. The bot lives inside Telegram, so there's no app to install, no account to create, and nothing new to learn.

And it keeps things contained. There's no public profile, no feed, no user directory. I don't see your phone number or your email — only a Telegram ID and the answers you've given. The one place another user might see you is a leaderboard, and only by the name you already use on Telegram.

Can I use this for NEET-PG or INI-CET, or is it only FMGE?

All three. The bank is a single pool of FMGE, NEET-PG and INI-CET recalls, and that's deliberate — an FMGE candidate practising a NEET-PG question is training the same skill.

Every question is tagged with the exam and the year it came from, so you always know what you're looking at.

What is a Grand Test?

A full-length mock in your exam's real shape: FMGE as 300 questions across two papers, NEET-PG and INI-CET with their own structure, timing and marking scheme.

There's a break you can take — it pauses the clock and costs marks, in the same way a break in the real exam costs you time you never get back. Afterwards you get a review of every question you missed.

Included with the plans — see pricing for how many each one carries.

Do I lose my progress if I don't upgrade?

No. Your streak, your accuracy by subject and your spaced-repetition schedule all stay exactly where they are.

A paid plan removes the daily limit — it doesn't unlock your own history.

Why aren't there video-based questions?

I want them. Video-based questions are simply hard to obtain legitimately.

I'm planning to ask faculty I know for help. And if you have access to any — or you teach — I'd genuinely like to hear from you.

Questions, accuracy and money

How do I know the answer keys are actually right?

Because they're checked by hand, and because I publish it when I get one wrong.

Every fix I've made is listed on the Fixes page — including the mistakes that were my own fault. If you spot something, there's a “This looks wrong” button on every question. It opens a real ticket you can follow with /myfeedback, so you can see when it's actually been fixed rather than wondering whether it went into a void.

Where do the questions come from?

Previous-year recalls from FMGE, NEET-PG and INI-CET, compiled and cleaned by hand.

Ciel Study is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the National Board of Examinations or any official examination body.

Is my payment information safe?

Payments go through Razorpay. Card and bank details are entered on their secure page — I never see them and they are never stored here.

What do you actually store about me?

Your Telegram ID and name, which questions you've answered and whether you got them right, your streak, and — if you've paid — a payment record.

That's it. No phone number, no email, no address. The details are in the Privacy Policy.

Will I be charged again automatically?

No. Every plan is a one-time payment and nothing renews on its own. No card mandate is created, so there is nothing to cancel — when the plan ends you simply go back to the free 5 questions a day.

There used to be an auto-renewing monthly option. It was withdrawn in August 2026 and nobody is on it. See the Refund & Cancellation Policy for refund windows.

Something not answered here? Send /feedback in the bot, or use the contact page. If it's a question more than one person asks, it ends up on this page.
