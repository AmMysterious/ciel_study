Payment method

Payments are processed by Razorpay. UPI, debit card, credit card and net banking are supported. Card and bank details are entered on Razorpay's secure payment page and are never seen or stored by Ciel Study.

How access is granted

Access is tied to the Telegram account that started the payment. Once the payment is confirmed by Razorpay, access is applied to that Telegram account automatically, usually within a few minutes. If your access has not been applied within 24 hours of a successful payment, contact us with your payment reference and it will be applied manually.

Auto-renewing plan. The ₹100 monthly plan sets up a recurring payment mandate through Razorpay. It will continue to charge ₹100 every 30 days until you cancel. You can cancel at any time from within the bot or by contacting us — see the Refund & Cancellation Policy.
