Pricing

All prices in Indian Rupees (INR ₹). Prices include all applicable taxes.

Free plan

Free — 3 questions per day, indefinitely. No payment details are required and there is no time-limited trial. The free allowance resets every day.

The free plan is not a stripped-down preview. Apart from the daily limit, it includes everything the paid plans include:

  • Every question carries a full written explanation.
  • Tutor mode and timed exam mode (10, 25 or 50 questions).
  • Image-based questions with the actual figure.
  • Spaced repetition — questions you answer incorrectly return on a schedule.
  • Accuracy tracking broken down by subject.
  • Daily streak and the weekly leaderboard.
  • Reporting a wrong answer key, and following the status of your report.

You can also earn unlimited access without paying: invite a friend and you and your friend each receive 3 days of unlimited access. There is no limit on the number of friends you may invite.

Payment method

Payments are processed by Razorpay. UPI, debit card, credit card and net banking are supported. Card and bank details are entered on Razorpay's secure payment page and are never seen or stored by Ciel Study.

How access is granted

Access is tied to the Telegram account that started the payment. Once the payment is confirmed by Razorpay, access is applied to that Telegram account automatically, usually within a few minutes. If your access has not been applied within 24 hours of a successful payment, contact us with your payment reference and it will be applied manually.

Auto-renewing plan. The ₹239 monthly plan sets up a recurring payment mandate through Razorpay. It will continue to charge ₹239 every 30 days until you cancel. You can cancel at any time from within the bot or by contacting us — see the Refund & Cancellation Policy.
