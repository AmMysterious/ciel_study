Community Medicine (PSM) · FMGE June 2026

A patient is diagnosed with plague. Which of the following is the vector responsible for transmission of the disease?

  • A. Louse
  • B. Flea
  • C. Sand fly
  • D. Mite

Answer B. Plague is carried by the rat flea, above all Xenopsylla cheopis.

Pharmacology · FMGE Set A

Which antiviral drug is a nucleoside reverse Transcriptase Inhibitor(NRTI) used in HIV infection?

  • A. Efavirenz
  • B. Ritonavir
  • C. Zidovudine
  • D. Maraviroc

Answer C. Zidovudine is a thymidine analogue and an NRTI. Watch for bone marrow suppression.

Community Medicine (PSM) · FMGE Set B

Which is incorrect regarding Scabies?

  • A. Scabies is also known as household infection
  • B. Scabies mainly affects the wrist and hand
  • C. Itching is worse during the daytime
  • D. Treatment include gamma HCH - lindane

Answer C. The itch of scabies is classically worse at night.

What "recall" means

NBEMS does not publish FMGE papers. Recall questions are rebuilt from what candidates remember after the exam, so wording can differ slightly from the real paper. Where we find a recall error, we correct it and say so on the question.
