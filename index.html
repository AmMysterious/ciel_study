Why this exists

Built because the PDFs weren't good enough

I'm sitting for FMGE again in January 2027. Somewhere between the fourth PDF dump and the tenth "recall" screenshot, I gave up and built the thing I actually wanted to study from.

About the answer keys. Not spot-checked — every question I touched, I verified the key against the vignette before writing the explanation. Doing that turned up real errors: questions keyed to the wrong option, explanations belonging to an entirely different question, and options with the answer accidentally pasted into them. Those are fixed. Every image-based question in the bank right now carries its own figure. Where I couldn't read one well enough to be certain, the question stays out of the bank rather than going in without it.

Questions

The things people ask first

Is it really free?

Yes — 5 questions every day, indefinitely, with no card and no countdown. There is no trial that expires. Paid plans only remove the daily limit.

Where do the questions come from?

Previous-year recalls from FMGE, NEET-PG and INI-CET, compiled and cleaned by hand. Ciel Study is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the National Board of Examinations or any official examination body.

What if an answer key is wrong?

Tell me — send /feedback in the bot and you'll get a ticket number. /myfeedback shows its status and any reply, so you can see when it's actually been fixed rather than wondering whether it went into a void.

Will I be charged again automatically?

No. Every plan is a one-time payment and nothing renews on its own. There is no card mandate and nothing to cancel — when the plan ends you simply go back to the free 5 questions a day. See the Refund & Cancellation Policy for refund windows.

Who runs this?

One person, not a company. I'm an FMGE candidate myself, sitting the exam again in January 2027. Contact details are on the contact page.
