Ciel Study

A previous-year question bank for FMGE aspirants, delivered through Telegram.

Ciel Study is a Telegram bot that serves verified previous-year multiple-choice questions for the Foreign Medical Graduate Examination (FMGE), along with NEET-PG and INI-CET recalls. Every question is served with a written explanation.

What you get

  • 3,300+ questions across 19 medical subjects
  • Tutor mode — one question at a time, with the explanation shown after each answer
  • Exam mode — timed 10, 25 or 50 question tests, scored at the end
  • Image-based questions — 310 questions that include the original figure
  • Spaced repetition — questions you answered incorrectly return on a schedule
  • Progress tracking — accuracy broken down by subject, plus a daily streak

How to use it

Open the bot on Telegram and send /start. You can practise 3 questions every day at no cost, with no time limit and no payment details required. If you want unlimited practice, paid plans are listed on the Pricing page.

Telegram bot: @Ciel_Study_bot

About the answer keys. The question keys in this bank have been checked by hand against the question stem before an explanation was written. Where an answer could not be verified with confidence, the question was left out rather than published with a guess. If you believe an answer is wrong, you can report it inside the bot with /feedback and follow the status of your report with /myfeedback.
