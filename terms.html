7a. Pass bonus — how a claim is verified

The 3-month and 6-month plans carry a pass bonus: 15% back on the 3-month plan, 25% back on the 6-month plan, of the amount you paid for that plan. Who qualifies is set out in section 2b of the Refund & Cancellation Policy. This section explains exactly how we check a claim, so you know what to send and what happens to it.

Step 1 — make the claim. Within 30 days of the result being declared, send /feedback pass bonus in the bot from the same Telegram account that bought the plan, or write to us from the contact page giving that Telegram username. A claim can only be paid on the account that paid for the plan.

Step 2 — prove the result. Send all of these:

  • Your official result or scorecard as downloaded from the NBEMS website (PDF or a clear screenshot), showing your name, roll number and the result — passed for FMGE, qualified for NEET-PG or INI-CET.
  • A short screen recording (under a minute) of you signing in to the official NBEMS candidate portal and opening that same result. A downloaded PDF can be edited; the live portal cannot, so the recording is what makes the claim verifiable.
  • Where NBEMS publishes a result list (FMGE results are published as a list of roll numbers), we also look your roll number up in that official list ourselves.

Step 2b — show that the result is yours. The NBEMS scorecard carries your name and photograph, so we check that the person claiming is the person on it. Send a selfie holding your masked Aadhaar — the version you download from the UIDAI website or the mAadhaar app that shows only the last 4 digits of the number. We compare your face and name with the scorecard. Please do not send a full Aadhaar number; the masked one is all we need.

The name on the result should match the name on the payment. If it does not (for example a family member paid), tell us who paid and we will ask the payer to confirm.

Step 3 — the practice record or the post. You need one of the two:

  • Regular practice: you do not need to send anything. We read your record in the bot: the number of days during the plan on which you answered at least 10 questions (Grand Test answers are not counted). It must reach one day in three of the plan — 30 days on the 3-month plan, 60 on the 6-month. We tell you the figure we found.
  • Or a public post about Ciel Study on Instagram, Telegram, Reddit or a similar public place: send us the link. It must be public, made from your own account, and still up when we check. We never ask for praise — write what you actually think — but if you post because of the bonus, say so in the post.

Step 4 — the decision. We reply within 7 working days of receiving everything: approved, or declined with the reason. If anything is unclear we ask before deciding. If you disagree with a decision, reply and we will look at it again.

Step 5 — payment. An approved bonus is refunded through Razorpay against the payment you made for the plan, within 7 working days of approval. Razorpay sends it back to the same UPI ID, card or bank account you paid from; we never see those details. Your bank may take a further 5–7 working days to show it. Razorpay only allows a refund for a limited time after a payment, so if that time has passed we send the bonus by UPI to an ID you give us. One bonus per plan bought.

Your documents. Result documents, recordings and the Aadhaar selfie are used only to check the claim. They are deleted within 30 days of the decision, never shared and never published. We do not name you or show your result anywhere without asking you first.

Misuse. A claim made with an altered or borrowed document, or someone else's result, is refused, and access may be ended under section 6. We would rather pay an honest claim quickly than make every student prove themselves twice, so please do not make us.
