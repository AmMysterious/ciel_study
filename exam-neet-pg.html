The NEET-PG Grand Test in the bot

  • 180questions
  • 210 minone sitting
  • +4 / −1marking
  • 40 markscost of a break

With −1 for a wrong answer, a blind guess costs you, so the Grand Test trains the real decision: answer what you know and leave what you don't. A blank scores 0. Questions are drawn from the NEET-PG and INI-CET recall bank, since the two exams share their question style; NBEMS publishes no subject weighting for NEET-PG, so the draw is not weighted.

NBEMS changed NEET-PG to 180 questions for 2026. The timing and marking above match it, but how the 180 are split into sections is not clearly published; the bot uses 4 sections of 45. If you know the official split, tell us with /feedback and we will correct it. A break costs 40 marks because a NEET-PG question is worth 4: it is the same ten questions' worth as on the other exams.

What a Grand Test feels like, step by step

  1. Choose the test. Send /gt and pick NEET-PG. Before anything starts, the bot shows the number of questions, the time, the marking and what a break costs, so nothing in your score is a surprise.
  2. The clock starts. Questions come one at a time with A, B, C, D buttons and a Skip. The time left is shown as you go. You do not see whether you were right, and there are no explanations, until you submit, the same as the hall.
  3. One way only. You cannot go back to a question you skipped or answered, so decide and move on. Closing Telegram does not stop the clock.
  4. Need a break? Tap the break button. Your clock pauses and 40 marks come off at once, about ten questions' worth. A break lasts at most 15 minutes, then the clock runs again whether you are back or not. In the real exam a bio break costs minutes you never get back; here you can buy them back, with marks.
  5. The result. Your marks out of the maximum, how many were right, wrong and left blank, each section's score, any break deduction, and how you compare with everyone who has taken the same test.
  6. The part that matters. Go through every mistake with its explanation. Each one goes into your Revise list, so the bot brings it back to you later until you get it right.

A sitting you leave open is scored automatically once the time it could have taken is over. Your past tests are in /gthistory. Grand Tests come with paid plans; see Pricing for how many each includes.
